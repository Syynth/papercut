this one

Sixteen tiles for Dirt meeting Meadow, straight from RPG Maker's autotile after conversion. Each tile is split into four quadrants, and a quadrant is tagged with the terrain it shows: the overlay is the tag. The outlined tile shows Meadow on its left and Dirt on its right.

2A corner is where four cells meet

NW cell · Meadow
NE cell · Dirt
SW cell · Meadow
SE cell · Dirt
the corner

On the map, tiles are not drawn per cell. They are drawn centred on the corners between cells, so each quadrant of a tile lands on one of the four cells around that corner. The tile chosen is the one tagged exactly like those four cells: Meadow, Dirt, Meadow, Dirt — the outlined tile from step 1.

So tagging is answering, per quadrant, "which terrain is under this part of the tile?" Pick Dirt, click the quadrant that shows dirt. Nothing is a real answer too: a quadrant that shows the edge of the ground, or the top of a cliff. Where on the sheet a tile sits never matters; only its four tags do.
