Against Tiled
What was cut, what stays
The first build put the tagger inside a 440 px box with a table's worth of readouts around it. Tiled's editor is the sheet, a list, a brush and undo; the extra pieces do not earn their place.
−
The 440 px box.

The section fills the window while it is open. A sheet at 100 % is the point; a quarter-scale thumbnail is not a tagger.

−
Fit / 1× / 2× / 4× segmented control.

Zoom is − / + and ⌘ wheel, with the percentage as the readout. One idea, where Tiled keeps it.

−
Counts in the list.

Corner counts and edge-set coverage go. The list is swatch and name. Coverage is read where it is decided: on the material, in Materials.

−
Name, Colour, Id, Edge set fields.

Double-click a terrain to rename it; click its swatch to recolour. The id is derived and never shown here.

−
The explanatory paragraph and the hint line.

One footer line over the sheet says what the brush is and what the buttons do. The rest is in the docs.

−
The Transitions block.

The “to author” list is the status bar's and the Materials section's. It never belonged under the sheet.

+
Undo and redo.

⌘Z / ⌘⇧Z and two buttons; a stroke, an add, a removal, a rename or a recolour is one step. Decided 2026-09-17.

+
Remove on the selected terrain.

The ✕ on the active row. Disabled, with a tooltip, while a material still uses it.

+
Nothing, as the first entry.

It is a real tag: the corner shows nothing. Right-click paints it too.
