The context, and the preview while tagging

The archetype in the bar is the working context for every view; the dropdown on the stage picks that archetype's slots. A small preview floats on the Tag stage, 2D or 3D, and updates as you tag.

Ramps

A ramp's surface is 1 × 1½, not √2: it sits on the half-tile grid and the mesher takes up the 6 %. It keeps the surface's corner tags, so blends, blocks, coverage and the preview all work on a slope. A rail is the ramp's trim, and the material picks its style: sheared or upright. Stairs are ramp art for now (tentative).

Still open

1 · Which way the long side runs

Steps seen head-on are not steps seen from the side turned a quarter. Proposed: two runs, 3@ramp down the sheet and 3@ramp-across, picked beside the slot, the mesher mirroring each for the opposite direction. To decide: two runs or four.

2 · Long cells on a square sheet

A 1 × 1½ cell does not fit a grid of whole tiles. To decide: may a sheet mix square and long cells, say as a region with its own cell, or does ramp art live on sheets of its own whose grid is 1 × 1½ throughout? The second is far simpler and costs one more image.

3 · Strips and the rest

To decide: do strip slots (fringe, picket, rail, seams) keep corner tags so they tile end to end, or are they tagged whole? And are Head, Foot and Side real slots, or does the ramp's surface carrying corner tags already answer head and foot, since the plateau and the ground are just its neighbours?
