To settle

1 · A slot has a size and a shape

A fringe is half a tile, a seam is a strip, a ramp's surface is longer than it is wide, a ramp's side is a triangle. Proposed: a slot declares its cell in half-tiles, and the tagger's grid and cursor follow the slot picked on the stage: pick Rail and the brush covers a 3 × 1 cell. To decide: does every slot keep four corner tags, so strips auto-tile end to end the way a surface does, or is a strip tagged whole?

2 · Trims on ramps: the rail

A fringe hangs off a cliff top and a picket stands at a wall's foot, both from art the floor already has. The ramp's version stands along its open sides. Proposed: Rail is a slot of the Ramp archetype with a height per material, on by default where a ramp's side is open, switched off per edge by the same brush that switches fringes off. To decide: a new slot, or the picket slot asked for by a ramp's edge, so one piece of art serves both?

3 · Which way the long side runs, and how long it is

Steps seen head-on are not steps seen from the side turned a quarter. A ramp that runs north–south wants a tile 1 × 1½; one that runs east–west wants 1½ × 1, and different art. Proposed: the ramp context has two runs, 3@ramp down the sheet and 3@ramp-across, picked beside the slot, and the mesher mirrors each for the opposite direction. And 1½ rather than √2: it sits on the half-tile grid every sheet already has, and the mesher takes up the 6 %. To decide: two runs or four, and whether a sheet may mix square and long cells or ramp art lives on sheets of its own.

Stairs

Tentative: stairs are ramp art for now, steps drawn on a ramp's tiles over a slope that stays smooth. A stair that steps its geometry, with treads the player stands level on, is a later question and would ask for the same art.
