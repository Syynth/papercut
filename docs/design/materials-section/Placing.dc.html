Spelling and placing transitions
A transition is spelled as its values, placed as a block, and the tags it writes are then plain corner tags on the sheet. Nothing is stored about the transition itself (ruling of 2026-09-17). The same control places the next one: swap a value, point again.

Pick a pairing in the subject bar and the values fill in: the later material by priority over the earlier. Or set them here.

2Point at its top-left tile

3Click: the tags land

Fifteen tiles are tagged at their corners, as if by hand. One undo step. The tags are the whole of it: the tile that is all Grass is left alone, because that tile is Grass's own.
