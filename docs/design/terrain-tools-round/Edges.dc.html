Edges: every trim in one place

A fringe, a picket and a rail are all the same kind of thing: art that stands on an edge of the terrain, put there by the material, with exceptions stored per edge. In Edges mode the terrain dims and every edge that can carry trim is drawn as a line, coloured by what it is doing, so the state that is invisible today can be seen and swept.

How it is used

The footprints work on edges. Brush along a cliff top; Rect or Lasso over a courtyard; Fill takes the whole connected rim of the plateau under the pointer, which is the common case in one click.

The filter says which trim: all of them, or fringes (cliff tops), pickets (wall feet) or rails (a ramp's open sides) alone. It filters both what is drawn and what a stroke touches.

On / Off / Auto is what the stroke writes. Auto forgets the exception, which is how an edge goes back to the material's say. Shift inverts On and Off.

Trim from names the material for a stroke of On, and defaults to the face's own. Alt samples it from an edge.

What moves

The Fringe verb leaves Paint mode and becomes this. The per-edge store the map already has carries fringes and pickets; rails and the material override are new fields in it. A rail's style and landings stay on the material, where they were put on 2026-09-19.

Open. Should Edges also carry the per-edge rail choices still undecided on the rails board: a rail that meets a fence on a plateau, and how far a rail stands out from its edge? They are edge facts, so they would live here if they are per edge at all.
