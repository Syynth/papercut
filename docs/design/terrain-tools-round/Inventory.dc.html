Terrain tools today: what each does, and what it cannot
An inventory before anything is proposed. Everything here is in the app now.
TodayWhat it doesWhat it cannot
Raise / LowerSets each column under the brush to its top ± strength, in half tiles. Shift lowers.A column is always solid from the floor to its top. Pressing a cliff face still moves tops.
FlattenSets columns to one height, sampled at the press or typed and pinned.Only ever a height. Cannot make a wall flush, or fill under an overhang.
SmoothMoves each column toward the mean of its four neighbours, by at most the strength.On a half-tile lattice it mostly rounds to nothing, then to a terrace. It never makes a slope.
RampDragged back from a cliff edge: cuts a run of 45° ramps, with a half ramp to finish an odd drop. Click a ramp to clear its run.The only way to make any shape. No slab, no single ramp, no stairs, no ramp that is not cut back from an edge.
FootprintsBrush (square or round, 1–12), rectangle from the press, contiguous fill.No line, no ellipse, no polygon. No volume at all: every footprint is a set of columns.
The strokeApplies once per cell as the pointer fully crosses into it, steered on the horizontal plane through the press, with a dead zone.Crossing back re-applies. Nothing shows what a press will do before it does it.
New facesA top or cliff face that sculpting creates copies a stack from a neighbour: a stopgap, written as one.Often the wrong material, and no say in it.
Fringes, pickets, railsAppear on their own wherever the material on top has the art. A Fringe brush in Paint mode switches single edges off and on.One edge at a time, one kind of trim, hidden in another mode. No way to see which edges carry what.

The mismatch

The map has been voxels since 2026-09-13: a cube a cell a layer, with a shape, and a stack of materials on every face. The mesher draws any of it: an overhang, a tunnel, a floating slab.

But every verb goes through one function that sets a column solid from the floor up to a height. The tools were designed for a heightfield and never redone, which the log says in as many words.

Your direction for this round. Keep the surface verbs for landscape and add a Block tool. New faces take a choice on the tool. And: extrude in directions other than up and down, various shape fills, better stroke feel, real tools for ramps and shapes, and one good tool for fringes.
