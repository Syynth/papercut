Selecting voxels: the island, the layer, the column, and a box
Proposed. All seen from the side.

Voxels: the island, and the other wholes

Island: connected, at or above the clicked layer
Layer: connected, on the clicked layer only
Column: straight down from the click
Same piece: a connected run of ramps
MatchTakesFor
IslandEvery voxel connected to the clicked one without going below its layer. Click a hill's top layer and you get its cap; click its lowest layer and you get the hill. The ground it stands on is never dragged in.moving, copying or deleting a hill, a building, a bridge
LayerThe connected voxels of the clicked layer alone: one storey.lifting a floor; carving a course
ColumnThe clicked voxel and everything under it, to the floor or to the first gap.pillars; what today's verbs think a cell is
Same pieceConnected voxels of the clicked one's shape: a run of ramps, a field of slabs.moving a staircase; turning slabs back to cubes

Triple-click drops the "not below" rule: everything connected, which on most maps is the whole volume, so it is one step further out and rarely what is wanted first.

Depth, for a drag — Surface, Through, and Box

Box: drag, then pull

Surface and Through are built. Box is the third: drag the footprint, release, move to set how far it reaches along the pressed face's normal, click. It is the only depth that can take air, which Fill and the shape fills will need, so a voxel region may hold empty cells when it was made with Box.

Seeing what is selected inside

A voxel region draws every voxel's sides, so a Through selection shows as a block. The layer view already hides layers above and below a range; a selection inside the hidden part stays selected and is drawn as a faint outline only, so nothing is ever selected invisibly.

Open. Is Island the right default for a double-click on a voxel, or should it be Layer? Island is what Move wants; Layer is safer, since it can never take more than one storey.
