The Sculpt verbs, redone

Raise by strength, once a stroke
Extrude: drag sets how far
Flatten to the pressed plane

Raise / Lower moves the footprint by the strength, along the pressed face's normal. Extrude is the same move with the distance taken from the drag: press, pull, and a number follows the pointer; type to set it exactly. Flatten fills and carves to the plane of the pressed face, so on a cliff it makes a wall flush.

Smooth: terraces, as now
Smooth with slopes on

Smooth gains a switch, Slopes: where relaxing leaves a step of a tile or less, it lays a ramp or a slab on it instead of a cliff. That is the smoothing a heightfield tool is expected to do, in the only vocabulary a voxel map has.

Stroke feel

RuleProposed
Ghost firstBefore the press, the footprint is drawn on the surface with what the press would add or remove as a ghost. Every verb, every mode. Nothing is a surprise.
Once a strokeA cell changes once in a stroke however the pointer wanders. Crossing back does nothing. To go further, press again, or raise the strength mid-stroke with the wheel.
Plane lockThe stroke stays on the plane of the pressed face, as it does now for tops, and now for cliffs too: a drag along a wall stays on that wall.
Cell crossingKept, with its dead zone: it is what makes a one-cell brush controllable. Size 2 and up apply on entering a cell, with no dead zone, since the footprint already overlaps.
Numbers on the stageExtrude distance, Flatten height and a Build shape's size read out beside the pointer while dragging.
ModifiersShift inverts (lower, carve, off). Alt samples: a height, a material, a piece. The same in every mode.

What a new face starts as — decided: a choice on the tool

Inherit: the surface keeps its paint as it moves. A raised top keeps its stack; the cliff it exposes takes the stack of the cliff it grew from, else the material's own choice of cliff, below. Active: every new face takes the palette's material on the palette's layer. A material gains an optional Cliffs are setting, the material its exposed sides start as, so raising grass exposes dirt without a second pass.
