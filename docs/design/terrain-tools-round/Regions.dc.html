Selecting voxels: the Region half of Select, and a region as a mask for every terrain verb
Proposed. Added at your prompt: nothing in the app today shows that a group of voxels can be selected.

Select › Region — decided 2026-09-12, never built: its verbs are greyed out in the bar today

The frame ruling made Select one tool over voxel regions and objects, with marquee, lasso and brush, and move, expand, contract and invert. Only the object half exists, and nothing on screen says a group of voxels can be selected at all. Proposed: the mode switch comes first in the bar, as every tool's does, and the region half is built on the same pieces as the rest of this round.

The footprint is the same control as in Terrain, on the plane of the face you press. Depth says how a flat footprint becomes a volume:

Surface: the voxels under the faces touched
Through: on down the normal, within the layer view
Box: footprint, then pull for depth; air counts
Fill: the connected solid, or the flat it is part of

Shift adds, alt subtracts, as the modifiers ruling already has it for objects. The layer view range on the viewport's right edge, also decided on 2026-09-12 and shipped as a slider that does nothing yet, bounds Through and Fill and hides what is outside it: it is how a region inside a hill or under a bridge is reached.

What a region is for

UseWhat it does
A maskWhile a region exists, every Terrain verb stays inside it: Sculpt, Build, Edges and Paint alike, as a selection does in a paint program. A chip in the Terrain bar shows it and clears it. This is the everyday use.
Move, DuplicateDrag the region along any axis, a tile at a time sideways and a half tile up and down. It leaves air behind; alt leaves a copy. Its faces keep their paint; the faces it uncovers follow the New faces switch.
Delete, FillCarve the region to air, or fill it solid with the picked Piece.
ExtrudePull the region's faces on one side out along their normal: the Sculpt verb, with the region as its footprint.
Expand, Contract, InvertGrow or shrink by one voxel in every direction, or only across the pressed plane; invert within the layer view.
Rotate, MirrorQuarter turns about the vertical, and a flip. Ramps turn with it.
Copy, PasteA copied region becomes a stamp: a Build shape placed against whatever face you press, Add or Carve. It could be kept in the project's library.
PaintPut the palette's material on every outward face of the region in one go.
