Faces: five meanings of "the whole"

Flat: connected and coplanar
Material: connected, same top material
Tile: connected, the same pasted tile
MatchTakesFor
FlatConnected faces in the clicked face's plane: same direction, same height or, for a wall, the same wall plane. Any material.extruding or raising a terrace; painting a whole wall
MaterialConnected faces whose visible material is the clicked face's, across planes and heights. A switch picks top layer or any layer.repainting a path, a meadow
TileConnected faces holding the same pasted tile.swapping every window of one kind
SurfaceConnected tops across changes of height up to a Step you set: 0 is Flat, ½ joins slabs and ramps, 1 joins a stair of terraces."the walkable ground here"
WallConnected side faces, round corners and up and down: the whole cliff.painting a building's or a cliff's faces

double-click a side face: that wall, in its plane
with Band on: only the clicked layer

On a side face, Flat is the wall in its own plane, every layer of it. A Band switch keeps it to the clicked layer: the course of a wall a trim or a row of windows goes on. With Wall instead of Flat, the band goes all the way round the building.
