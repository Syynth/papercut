The proposal in one board: four modes, one footprint, and the pressed face sets the direction
Proposed. The Sculpt verbs stay and stop meaning "up"; Build adds volume; Edges gathers every trim; Paint is unchanged.

Footprint is one control, the same in every mode: the set of faces, cells or edges a press takes. Line, Ellipse and Lasso are new everywhere at once, so a fringe can be switched off along a lasso as easily as a hill is raised with one.

One rule: the face you press sets the direction

Today every verb means "up". Instead, the verb acts along the normal of the face under the pointer at the press, and the stroke stays on that face's plane. Press a top and Raise lifts. Press a cliff and the same Raise pushes the cliff out; with shift it pulls it in. Nothing new to learn, and that is extrusion in all six directions.

press a top: up
press a cliff: out
shift: in, which carves
on an overhang’s top
on its underside: down

Out and in move a whole tile at a time, because a voxel is a cube; up and down keep the half tile, which is a slab. A surface is the run of solid the press landed on, not the column from the floor: raising a bridge lifts the bridge, not the ground under it.
