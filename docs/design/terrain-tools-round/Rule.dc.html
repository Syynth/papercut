Select, then act: the terrain verbs with the selection as the footprint
Redrawn 2026-09-21, now that selection, Move and the span mesher are built. Proposed, on top of two decisions: the selection drives the terrain tools, and the sculpt tools are iterated one at a time from use. It replaces the board of four modes and one shared Footprint control.

The rule — a verb is one operation on a set of elements

Select gives the verb the set you chose, and the verb gets a handle, a ghost and a number. Terrain gives it the set under the brush, one press at a time, and clips it to the selection when there is one. It is the same operation either way, with the same paint on new faces and the same undo. Footprint stops being a control on every mode and becomes how you select.

At once, or dragged

Delete, Fill, Flatten, Smooth and Paint happen on the click or the key. Move, Extrude and Ramp show a handle on the face pressed: drag it or type a number, the result is a ghost until release, and Escape puts it back.

After a verb

The selection follows the result. Extruded faces stay selected where they end up, so the next drag carries on. Deleted voxels leave nothing selected. Undo brings back the terrain and the selection togetherbuilt

Extrude — the selected faces set the direction

This is the old board's one rule, kept: a verb acts along the normal of the face. It no longer hangs on where the pointer happened to press, because the faces are chosen first. Up and down move by the half tile, which is a slab; out and in by the whole tile, because a voxel is a cube. A surface is the run of solid the faces belong to, not the column: extruding a bridge's top lifts the bridge and leaves the ground under it.

Terrain keeps its brush — a press is a selection that lasts one press

plan: the brush (green and dashed) over a selection (amber)

With nothing selected, Terrain works as it does now: pick a verb, press, drag, and the ground under the brush changes. Underneath it is the same operation on the faces under the brush, so Raise is Extrude by the strength and acts on the run pressed. With a selection, the brush only changes what is inside it: select a plateau by Surface, then paint or raise freely without spilling over its rim. The bar says so while it is true, and Escape clears it.

What this does to the four modes

Sculpt, Build and Paint stay as the brush-first ways in. Edges was going to be a mode of its own so that trims could be swept with a footprint; edges are now something you select, so it becomes the Trim verb on an edge selection, and a Trim brush in Paint for the quick case. Build's shape fills are the one thing that starts from nothing: they make a selection's worth of voxels rather than act on one.
