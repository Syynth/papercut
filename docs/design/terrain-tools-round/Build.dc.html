A shape is drawn in two moves, the way a box is in any 3D tool: drag the footprint on the pressed face's plane, release, then move to set how far it stands out, and click. The Height field holds the last value, so a second box is one drag. Because the pressed face sets the plane, a Box dragged on a cliff is a balcony; Carve on a cliff is a doorway or a tunnel.

Block — one voxel at a time

Click a face to put a piece against it; shift-click removes the piece under the pointer. Drag paints along the pressed plane. The Piece picker says what is placed: a cube, a slab, a ramp or a half ramp. A ramp points the way the drag goes, or turns with R. Alt samples a piece from the map.

Seeing inside — the layer view, decided 2026-09-12

Overhangs hide what is under them. The frame ruling already has the answer: a layer view range on the viewport's right edge, an upper and a lower bound, that hides every layer outside it. It shipped as a slider that does nothing yet. This rework wires it: PgUp / PgDn step it, and it bounds what Fill and a region reach. It changes what is drawn, never the map.
