Open questions, and an order to build in
What I need your call on before any of this is built. Comment on the boards, or answer here.
Is the pressed face the right rule?
It makes Raise on a cliff push the cliff out. The alternative is a separate Extrude-only tool and Raise that always means up. One rule is less to learn; the risk is pushing a wall out when you meant to lift the ground beside it, which the ghost is there to catch.
Is a surface the run of solid you pressed, or the column?
With overhangs a column can hold two surfaces. Proposed: the run you pressed. Lowering a bridge to nothing removes the bridge and leaves the ground.
Four modes, or fewer?
Sculpt, Build, Edges, Paint. Build could fold into Sculpt as more verbs, at the cost of a very long bar. Edges could stay in Paint, at the cost of staying hidden.
Which shape fills first?
Drawn: Box, Wall, Cylinder, Dome, Stairs, each Add or Carve, Solid or Hollow. Not drawn: pyramid, cone, arch, torus, a lathe from a sketch profile. Which of these earn a place?
Whole tiles sideways?
Out and in move a tile at a time because a voxel is a cube. A half-tile step sideways would be a new shape in all four directions. Proposed: no.
A material names its cliffs?
"Cliffs are: Dirt" on Grass, used when Inherit exposes a side with nothing to copy. Small, and it removes the most common second pass.
Smooth with slopes on by default?
It changes what Smooth has always done here. On by default makes it useful at once; off keeps old maps behaving the same under the same stroke.
Is a region voxels, or faces?
On the Regions board. Proposed: voxels, read as "the faces of these voxels" when it masks Paint and Edges.

A build order, if this is right

#Piece of workWhy there
1The document's operations become voxel operations: add, remove and move a run of solid along an axis, with faces reconciled by Inherit or Active.Everything else calls these. Today's verbs are rewritten on top and behave the same on a plain hill.
2The pressed face sets the direction; ghosts; once a stroke.Extrusion in six directions falls out of step 1, and the feel changes land together.
3The new footprints: Line, Ellipse, Lasso.Shared by every mode, so they are built once.
4Select › Region, the layer view wired, and the region as a mask.Needs 3. Early, because a mask makes every later verb more useful and Move needs only step 1.
5Build: Block and the Piece picker, then the shape fills; a copied region as a stamp.Needs 1, 3 and 4.
6Ramp between two heights, Reshape, Smooth with slopes.Needs the pieces from 5.
7Edges mode, and the material override.Independent of 1–6; could go first if fringes hurt most.
