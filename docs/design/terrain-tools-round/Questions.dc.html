Open questions, and an order to build in
Redrawn 2026-09-21 around the selection. Amber is new, blue is still open from the first round, green has been answered by a decision or by what was built. Comment on a board, or answer here.
Where do the verbs live?new
Proposed: in both places, as one operation. Select › Region grows a verb row beside Move; Terrain keeps its brush and calls the same operation on what is under it. The alternative is verbs only in Select, which makes quick sculpting two steps.
Does a selection mask the Terrain brush?new
Proposed: yes, always, with the bar saying so and Escape clearing it. The risk is a forgotten selection somewhere off screen making the brush seem dead; the selection pill in the corner is there to catch that.
Faces pointing different ways, extruded together?new
Proposed: each along its own normal, and the corner between a top and a side is left open, as drawn. The alternative is to extrude only the faces that point the way of the one pressed.
What does Delete mean on faces and edges?new
On voxels it removes them. Proposed: on faces it clears the active material layer, and on edges it switches their trim off, so the key always means "take this away". Or it is a voxel verb only.
What is selected after a verb?new
Proposed: the result. Extruded faces at their new place, moved voxels where they land, nothing after a Delete.
A half-tile step sideways?still open
Out and in move a whole tile because a voxel is a cube. A half step sideways would be a new shape in all four directions. Proposed: no.
Which shape fills first?still open
Drawn on Build: Box, Wall, Cylinder, Dome, Stairs, each Add or Carve, Solid or Hollow. Not drawn: pyramid, cone, arch, a lathe from a sketch profile. Better answered once Delete, Fill and Extrude have been used for a while.
A material names its cliffs?still open
"Cliffs are: Dirt" on Grass, used when Inherit bares a side with nothing to copy. It matters more now: a lifted block came out part grass, part dirt, and magenta where the set had no tile for that.
Smooth with slopes on by default?still open
On makes it useful at once; off keeps old maps behaving the same under the same stroke.
Is a region voxels, or faces?answered
Edges, faces or voxels, chosen on the bar (decided 2026-09-20, built).
Is a surface the run you pressed, or the column?answered
The run. The mesher draws runs, and picks, selection and paint already act on the one pressed (built 2026-09-21). The sculpt verbs are the last thing still filling a column solid; step 3 fixes that.
Is the pressed face the right rule?answered
Kept, and safer than it was: the faces are chosen before the verb acts, so a wall cannot be pushed out by a press that landed a pixel off the ground.
Four modes, or fewer?answered
Three and a verb: Edges becomes Trim on an edge selection. See the Rule board.

A build order from here — each step usable on its own

#Piece of workWhy there
1Delete and Fill on a voxel selection. Under them, the document's add and remove for any set of voxels, with new faces by Inherit or Active.The smallest thing that lets you carve a cave or swap cubes for slabs, and every later verb calls the same two operations.
2Extrude on a face selection: handle, ghost, a typed distance, dragged back to carve.Six directions in one verb. It is add and remove from step 1, aimed by the faces.
3The Terrain brush goes through the same operations: a press is a one-press selection, a standing selection masks it, and Raise, Lower and Flatten act on the run pressed.Ends the last place a gap gets filled solid, and gives paint-inside-a-selection.
4Flatten, Smooth with slopes, and Ramp between two levels, as verbs on a face selection.Needs 2. Order among them by what hurts.
5Line, Ellipse and Lasso footprints; Box depth; converting a selection between elements.Independent. Earlier if selecting is the slow part.
6Build's shape fills, Copy as a stamp, Rotate and Mirror.Needs 1.
7Trim on an edge selection, and trim from a named material.Independent. First if fringes hurt most.

Already built, from the old order's step 4: Region select on three elements with Match, Move with handles and nudge, undo that restores the selection, tool state that survives a reload, and a mesher that draws what the voxels hold.
