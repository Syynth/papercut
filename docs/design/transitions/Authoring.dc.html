Pointing at a block

Click a tile on any sheet and papercut takes it as the block's top-left. The outline shows what it would claim, and the shape is known already from the material count, so a wrong click is visible before it is saved.

Nothing about the block is stored but its corner: the tiles are read out of the image every time the project opens, so repainting the art needs no retagging at all.

Overloads

Two transitions may name the same materials and differ in orientation — the row above has floor and wall blocks for Grass + Wood. Where grass runs up to a wooden wall, the top faces take the first and the sides take the second.

A transition marked any answers whatever no more specific one does, which is what a kit with one look for both would use.
