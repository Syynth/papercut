Feeling it out · the split

Shape asks one question, material answers another

"Grass against nothing" is not grass meeting a material called nothing — it is grass at a shape. Separating the two makes inside corners, outside corners and edge tiles stop being special cases to bolt on: they were never material combinations. An archetype is the shape vocabulary a material owes art for, and papercut ships the set.

Most art is the first kind. The combinatorial explosion that made a 21-terrain sheet 96 % void was caused by treating every pairing as something that must exist; here a pairing exists only when somebody draws it, and everything else composites.

Where this comes from
•

Corner Wang tiles — the formal name for the dual grid papercut already uses.

•

Blob tiling, the 47-tile set — matches all eight neighbours, so it separates inside from outside corners. RPG Maker's A2 autotile is a compact encoding of it.

•

Tiled's Wang sets come in corner, edge and mixed kinds; Godot's tileset terrains match corners, sides, or both. Both tools make the same split: which positions carry a label.

•

Ferr2D — already named in structure.ts for papercut's own fill-and-edge sketch material. Shape-indexed art is in the codebase's vocabulary already.
