If any art is chosen by something other than the four materials at a corner, then a tile can be tagged two different ways and the model needs both:

A corner tag
"the four cells here are stone, stone, stone, nothing"

Fifteen of these per pairing. Derived from a block's position — nobody types them.

A shape tag
"this tile IS stone's convex seam"

A handful per material, named by the archetype. The artist points at a tile and picks which named slot it fills.
